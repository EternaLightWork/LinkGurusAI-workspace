Business implementation is complete when:

Roles are active; authority is usable; the workflow has changed; business controls are applied; and a specific named person from the client has the capacity and power to own the result and intervene.

يكتمل تطبيق جانب الأعمال عندما:

Different from Portfolio Master

Portfolio Master is the 3×3 commercial and diagnostic map: it helps a buyer identify the right entry/flagship/scale route and carries one completion promise across nine offers.

Job of this visual

Agentic Organization Master explains the strategic thesis, six-stage 6A movement, business/technology boundary, the three practices’ roles, and how value travels through human–agent work.

Recommended use: yes, the simplified upper system is client-facing—homepage “how we work,” proposal opening, executive workshop pre-read, and thought-leadership diagrams. The comparison notes and internal governance language should be removed from the public export. Portfolio Master remains a separate client-facing diagnostic/lead magnet; the two visuals should not be merged.
