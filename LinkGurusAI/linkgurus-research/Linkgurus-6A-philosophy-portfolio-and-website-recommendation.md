# Linkgurus 6A Philosophy — Portfolio and Website Recommendation

**Purpose.** Decide whether the Linkgurus 6A philosophy should be kept or discarded, identify where it fits within the settled service portfolio, and define how—if at all—it should appear on the website.

**Recommendation in one sentence.** Keep 6A as the large-enterprise delivery path, primarily housed under **Restructuring & Org. Design** and commercially led by **The Install — إرساء النموذج المؤسسي**; it is not a fourth practice, a tenth offer, or a replacement for any of the nine offers.

**Evidence language.** FACT = established in the project record. INFERENCE = a reasoned strategic conclusion. HYPOTHESIS = requires delivery or buyer testing. UNKNOWN = evidence is not yet available.

---

## 1. Decision

**Keep the philosophy; retire its current external form.**

- **Keep:** the final six-stage logic—Awaken, Assess, AI Fit, Anchor, Align and Ascend—the meaning of the names, and the diagnosis-to-implementation sequence.
- **Retire:** the current screenshot and framework inventory as website or sales content.
- **Do not create:** a fourth practice, a tenth offer, or a separate 6A consulting product.
- **Develop:** a repeatable delivery method with distinct instruments, artifacts, decisions, and completion conditions.
- **Test first:** within **البنية والتصميم المؤسسي**, especially **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي**.

This treatment preserves the potentially useful method without increasing the portfolio breadth and credibility risks identified in the final launch roadmap.

---

## 2. Evidence assessment

### FACT

1. **Final founder decision:** the named sequence is Awaken, Assess, AI Fit, Anchor, Align and Ascend. AI Fit is the third A and a full stage in the 6A philosophy.
2. The current contents primarily organize public frameworks and tools, including SWOT, PESTEL, McKinsey 7S, Balanced Scorecard, OKRs, Lean, Six Sigma and similar methods.
3. Phase 1 classified the 6A's as **REPACKAGE**: a potentially useful container, but not defensible IP in its current form.
4. The settled Linkgurus architecture contains three practices, nine offers and the T7 implementation-accountability spine.
5. The stress-tested roadmap explicitly recommends against adding another offer or presenting the existing portfolio as a flat catalogue.

### INFERENCE

1. Publishing the current matrix would make Linkgurus appear broader and more generalist than the final portfolio intends.
2. Listing familiar frameworks would emphasize the public ingredients rather than Linkgurus's diagnostic judgment or delivery outcomes.
3. The strongest commercial fit is Practice 2 because its offer ladder already moves from diagnosis to authority transfer, enactment and review.
4. The 6A sequence can add value as a delivery architecture only when each stage produces a distinct decision or operating artifact.

### HYPOTHESIS

1. A demonstrated six-stage method may reduce perceived delivery risk once it is supported by a referenceable engagement.
2. The system may eventually operate across both Practice 2 and Practice 3.
3. Clients may remember or value the 6A naming once it is connected to concrete outcomes rather than public frameworks.

### UNKNOWN

1. Whether Shadow Mapping, Org Dysfunction Mapping and Decision Rights Mapping are genuinely original instruments or labels for conventional practice.
2. Whether the English 6A naming will be commercially useful on an Arabic-first website.
3. Whether clients will perceive six distinct stages or see them as an unnecessary layer over the simpler T7 promise.

## 3. Correct position in the Linkgurus architecture

The portfolio hierarchy should be:

| Layer | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Corporate promise** | «مَن يقرر… وماذا يبقى» |
| **Three practices** | المختبر · البنية والتصميم المؤسسي · نموذج التشغيل الذكي |
| **Nine offers** | Entry · flagship · scale |
| **T7 promise line** | Organization design is not complete until it is applied and change is managed |
| **6A delivery path** | How Linkgurus moves the client from the initial problem to that operating state |
| **Instruments** | The maps, decision records, role designs, registers and hold checks produced during delivery |

The relationship between the T7 promise and 6A should be stated simply:

> **Organization design is not complete until it is applied and change is managed. 6A is the path Linkgurus uses to deliver that promise.**

The 6A's therefore sit underneath the offers, not alongside them.

## 4. Primary portfolio fit

### Primary home: البنية والتصميم المؤسسي

The strongest fit is **Practice 2: البنية والتصميم المؤسسي**, particularly **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي / The Install**.

| Portfolio level | Relationship to the 6A's |
|---|---|
| **خارطة الصلاحيات** | **Awaken + Assess:** surface the decision problem, trace latency and establish the baseline |
| **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي** | The complete 6A journey: diagnose, establish authority, redesign roles and workflows, enact the change and verify use |
| **فحص الصلاحيات الدوري** | **Ascend as a recurring loop:** inspect drift, correct it and confirm that authority still holds |

### Proposed delivery interpretation

| Stage | Final Arabic wording | Commercial meaning | Possible evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awaken** | **نكشف موضع التعطل** | Identify the real trigger and operating problem | Trigger statement and decision-path baseline |
| **Assess** | **نثبت خط الأساس** | Diagnose where roles, authority and workflows break | Dysfunction, collision or authority map |
| **AI Fit** | **هل يدخل الذكاء الآن؟** | Decide Now / Later / No only after the baseline is assessed | Recorded fit decision, dependencies and dated backlog where relevant |
| **Anchor** | **نثبت من يقرر وما الذي يتغير** | Establish owners, operating principles and authority thresholds | Named-owner and decision-rights architecture |
| **Align** | **نُدخل التصميم في العمل** | Redesign roles, workflows and operating relationships in live work | Role charters and operating-model blueprint |
| **Ascend** | **نثبت ما تم تصميمه** | Launch, stabilize and verify that the design holds | Enactment register and T7 hold check |

Large projects begin as restructuring and organization-design engagements. AI is introduced only after the baseline is assessed. The AI Fit Gate records one of three decisions:

- **Now:** embed AI inside the same target operating model.
- **Later:** prepare interfaces, dependencies and a dated backlog.
- **No:** proceed with restructuring without artificial AI content.

AI must not become a parallel technology plan.

This is materially stronger than presenting the 6A's as a catalogue of consulting frameworks.

| Practice | Fit | Recommendation |

The 6A's could eventually become a firm-wide delivery system across Practices 2 and 3. That status should follow proof from delivery rather than precede it.

## 6. What to keep, rebuild and retire

### Keep

- The final six-stage sequence.
- The personal meaning behind the names.
- The movement from diagnosis to application and continuing review.
- The possibility of housing genuinely original instruments inside the stages.
- “6A” as an internal working name while the method is developed.

### Rebuild

- Define a unique purpose and phase gate for every stage.
- Give each stage a standard input, procedure, decision and client artifact.
- Connect Ascend explicitly to enactment and managed change under the T7 promise line.
- Document the method so someone other than the founder could follow it.
- Verify the origin and ownership of every instrument placed inside it.

### Retire from external use

- The current screenshot and spreadsheet-style presentation.
- Public lists of SWOT, PESTEL, McKinsey 7S, Balanced Scorecard, OKRs, Lean, Six Sigma and similar frameworks.
- The broad “Org Domain” catalogue, which implies additional culture, leadership, succession, operational-excellence and performance-management service lines.
- Any description such as “our proprietary 6A methodology” before originality and repeatability are demonstrated.
- Positioning the 6A's as a practice, standalone offer or separate consulting product.

The current artifact should be archived as source material, not deleted. The external version should be rebuilt around decisions, artifacts and operating outcomes.

## 7. Website placement

### Do not place it here

- Not in the homepage hero.
- Not as a fourth practice card.
- Not among the offer cards.
- Not as a large “Our Philosophy” framework grid.
- Not as proof that Linkgurus owns a proprietary methodology.

### Recommended placement

1. **Homepage:** a compact “How we work” section after the three practices and their current availability status.
2. **Practice 2 page:** the complete six-stage journey, applied specifically to إرساء النموذج المؤسسي.
3. **Proposals and statements of work:** the detailed version with inputs, decisions, phase gates, artifacts and completion conditions.
4. **About/method page:** a short account of the meaning of Awaken, Assess, AI Fit, Anchor, Align and Ascend without making the corporate brand founder-dependent.

The philosophy's emotional meaning belongs primarily in the founder story. Its commercial meaning must come from what each stage changes for the client.

### Recommended Arabic-first presentation

Do not force the English alliteration into Arabic. Lead with a functional Arabic explanation:

> **كيف نعمل: من تشخيص موضع التعطل إلى تطبيق بنية تعمل فعلاً**

The visible delivery path, including the AI Fit Gate, is:

1. **نكشف موضع التعطل** — Awaken.
2. **نثبت خط الأساس** — Assess.
3. **هل يدخل الذكاء الآن؟** — AI Fit Gate: Now / Later / No.
4. **نثبت من يقرر وما الذي يتغير** — Anchor.
5. **نُدخل التصميم في العمل** — Align.
6. **نثبت ما تم تصميمه** — Ascend.

The T7 line should close the section:

> **لا يكتمل التصميم المؤسسي إلا بالتطبيق وإدارة التغيير.**

“6A” may appear as a small method label later. It should not be the primary Arabic headline.

## 8. Publication sequence

The method's website visibility should follow the proof-gated launch roadmap.

| Roadmap stage | Treatment of the 6A's |
|---|---|
| **Stage 0 — Evidence readiness** | Internal development only. Define the stages, ownership and instruments. |
| **Stage 1 — Quiet paid pilots** | Use privately in proposals and delivery. Do not make a public method claim. |
| **Stage 2 — Evidence-led soft transition** | Publish a simplified “How we work” section only if the first engagement demonstrates that the stages genuinely organize delivery. |
| **Stage 3 — Public proof launch** | Name and explain the 6A method only after an enacted flagship provides a real case connecting the stages to observed operating change. |
| **Stage 4 — Scale** | Use it for delegation, training or licensing only after a second person can execute it to a defined quality standard. |

---

## 9. IP and proof gates

The 6A's become more than packaging only when every stage has:

1. a distinct decision gate;
2. a repeatable procedure;
3. a named client artifact;
4. an observable completion condition;
5. at least one instrument that is genuinely Linkgurus-owned;
6. sufficient documentation for someone other than the founder to execute it;
7. evidence from a delivered engagement that the stage changed what the client did.

The highest-value unresolved question remains whether **Shadow Mapping, Org Dysfunction Mapping and Decision Rights Mapping** are original instruments or labels for conventional consulting work. This determines whether the 6A container can become genuine IP.

---

## 10. Keep-or-discard test

Review the 6A system after two or three relevant engagements.

### Keep and elevate it if

- clients can distinguish the six stages;
- each stage produces a different decision or artifact;
- skipping a stage creates a known delivery failure;
- the stages lead to enactment and a measurable hold check;
- a second practitioner can follow the method;
- at least one instrument has verified original ownership.

### Discard the public 6A label if

- the stages repeatedly overlap or need lengthy explanation;
- the method adds no clarity beyond the T7 promise line;
- the value continues to come from founder judgment that cannot be documented;
- clients remember the outcome but find the six-stage language irrelevant;
- the English naming feels imposed on the Arabic-first buyer experience;
- no distinct or ownable instruments emerge.

Discarding the public label would not require abandoning useful delivery discipline. The stages could remain internal while the T7 promise remains public.

---

## Final recommendation

**Do not discard the philosophy. Discard its current external form.**

Keep it as an internal method-development asset, make **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي** its first proving ground, and allow it to earn promotion to a public Linkgurus method through actual delivery evidence.

If the first two or three engagements show that the stages have no distinct instruments, decisions or commercial value beyond the simpler T7 promise, retire the public 6A name. Until then, the correct action is **rebuild and test**, not publish as-is and not discard prematurely.
