Linkgurus — Stress-Tested Alternative Launch Roadmap#

Purpose. This is an alternative to Phase12-linkgurus-launch-roadmap.md, cross-checked against the Phase 11 white-paper evidence stress test and the decisions in Phases 8–10. It does not silently overwrite a founder decision. Where it challenges D7, D11/D13, or the current visibility sequence, it presents a decision for the founder.

Recommendation in one sentence. Keep the portfolio and its names; launch it as a proof-gated system rather than a nine-offer menu: sell three bounded entry offers quietly, foreground the externally well-supported القيمة المعلّقة, use خارطة الصلاحيات → إرساء النموذج المؤسسي as the first full flagship path, and do not publicly present all nine offers as equally available until one flagship has reached an evidenced operating state.

Evidence language. FACT = established in the project record or cited white papers; INFERENCE = reasoned consequence; HYPOTHESIS = requires a transaction or buyer test; UNKNOWN = founder or market input not present in the files.

Bottom-line verdict on the existing Phase 12#

Phase 12 is directionally sound on proof before publicity, founder anchoring, the first full flagship, and delaying the AI Operating Model sale. It is weakest where it treats the portfolio as ready for equal public presentation, leaves the best-supported entry need too late, and uses generic market-need evidence as if it were closer to offer proof than it is. The alternative below corrects those points without renaming, killing, or merging an offer.

What changes in the tree—and what does not#

  • Keep: three practices, nine Arabic names, English working names, buyers, T7 spine, Linkgurus corporate name, founder-anchored voice, and no automatic discounting.
  • Change: move Value on Hold into the first selling wave; distinguish point of view from sellable offer; show availability gates; require delivery proof before a full public launch; make T7 measurable.
  • Do not add a tenth offer. A new “activation” or “value realization” product would create more naming and scope work before any transaction exists. The existing Value on Hold → AI Operating Model ladder is sufficient to test the need.
  • Founder decision required: D7 currently says all nine offers are announced together. The proposed alternative preserves all nine but does not present all nine as equally buyable at soft transition. Approve or reject that distinction explicitly.

1. Flagship offer packaging#

1.1 First full flagship#

Recommendation: إرساء النموذج المؤسسي (The Install), entered through خارطة الصلاحيات (Where Decisions Stop).

This is not because the external papers validate a GCC boutique offer called “The Install.” They do not. It leads because four factors intersect:

  1. Founder capability is strongest here in the internal evidence.
  2. The offer ladder is the clearest: map authority → transfer authority → inspect authority.
  3. The broad need is strongly supported: roles, workflows, decision rights, accountability, and enactment recur across Accenture, McKinsey, and Oliver Wyman.
  4. It has a lower explanation burden than the agentic-AI flagship. This is an inference from familiar authority vocabulary, not proof that buyers will purchase the offer.

1.2 Buyer, trigger, scope, and delivery model#

Element Packaging recommendation
Primary buyer CEO, owner, or board sponsor with authority to change roles and decision rights. An HR-only sponsor without CEO authority is a poor fit because the engagement requires authority to move, not only designs to be approved.
Buyable trigger Two or more high-value decisions repeatedly route back to one person; a new structure has been approved but not enacted; or growth has made the current authority system unusable. These are hypotheses to validate in sales, not market-size claims.
Entry خارطة الصلاحيات, one week: trace 2–3 real decisions, baseline latency and handoffs, identify the top authority bottlenecks, and sequence the consequences. It remains valid standalone.
Flagship format Fixed-price project with facilitated executive decisions inside it. It is neither a workshop product nor a retainer. Facilitation is a delivery mechanism; the product is an operating state.
Duration 10–14 weeks, retained from Phase 8.2. This is a proposed delivery band, not externally validated. Scope must be bounded tightly enough to make the band real.
Core work Decision and authority baseline; job architecture; spans and layers; role definitions; decision-rights and authority thresholds; executive resolution sessions; implementation/change plan; enactment; hold check.
Completion condition At least two material authority thresholds have been transferred, used in live decisions, and observed against the baseline. “Board approved” or “org chart signed” is not completion.
Explicit exclusions Total rewards, salary scales, technical implementation, legal opinions, and regulatory assurance. Any adjacent need is separately scoped or referred.
Tail فحص الصلاحيات الدوري is offered only after the first hold check. It should be separately chosen and priced so that repeat demand is measured rather than created by compulsory bundling.

1.3 Deliverables#

  1. Authority baseline: decision-path and latency map for 2–3 material decisions.
  2. Role and decision architecture: role charters, decision owners, approval thresholds, escalation and override rules.
  3. Enactment register: what moved, from whom, to whom, when, and on which live decision it was exercised.
  4. Operating evidence pack: before/after measures, decision examples, unresolved risks, and the next review date.
  5. Permissioned case-study record: client name and outcome language agreed separately. Permission is requested in the SOW; it is not a condition of good delivery.

1.4 Price range and evidence honesty#

Retain the existing $55,000–$90,000 range (approximately SAR 205,000–340,000 at the fixed 3.75 conversion used in Phase 8.2), but label it internally and externally as a fixed-scope project range—not as a proven market rate.

Available pricing signal What it legitimately supports What it does not support
GCC independent-consultant benchmark: $600–$1,800/day Shows the tier Linkgurus is trying to escape by pricing a defined product rather than time. Does not validate a $55k–$90k project.
AI-native boutique engagements: $75k–$500k Bounds a different, AI-oriented boutique category and supports caution about enterprise consulting ticket sizes. Not a comparable org-design rate card.
DSA/DesignThinkers training signals: €2,500/person and an inferred €35k–€40k 15-person cohort Useful for capability-transfer offers such as مختبركم. Not relevant evidence for إرساء النموذج المؤسسي.
Competitor org-design prices No usable public price evidence found. No competitor-based claim can be made for this range.
Phase 8.2 founder decision and internal scope logic Provides the only current anchor and avoids inventing a new number. Does not prove willingness to pay.

What I would change from Phase 12’s price defence: remove the unsourced $150k–$250k COO compensation figure from the argument. “Hiring a senior operator” can remain a qualitative alternative identified in the buyer research, but a compensation number outside the evidence base should not make the price appear researched.

Range mechanics—proposal for founder approval: the low end should correspond to one bounded unit or a narrow decision system; the high end to multi-function role/authority redesign with greater enactment complexity. Exact scope variables, payment milestones, travel treatment, cash runway, and the walk-away floor are founder-only inputs. Do not make the first buyer discover the floor during negotiation.

D11/D13 must be resolved before an Egypt pilot. The record simultaneously says “no founding-client discount” and describes Egypt as case-study ground priced as founding-client work. Choose one rule: full GCC list price everywhere, or a documented non-price exchange/limited pilot policy. Do not improvise a discount in the first negotiation.

2. Proof-before-scale sequencing#

2.1 What proof means#

Proof must establish Linkgurus’s approach, not merely repeat a global trend. For the flagship, credible proof contains:

  • a measured before-state;
  • a material authority or role change;
  • evidence that the change was used in a live decision;
  • a 30/60/90-day hold observation where timing permits;
  • a named or otherwise verifiable client reference;
  • clear boundaries on what Linkgurus did and did not cause.

An approved design, a satisfaction quote, attendance, an unnamed logo-free anecdote, or a report delivered is insufficient on its own.

2.2 Recommended first 2–3 proof points#

Proof point A — verified legacy credibility, before public claims#

Close the two cheapest evidence gaps: quantify the GISCON outcome if the client can confirm it, and verify the founder’s enterprise-transformation scope through a document or former colleague. This does not become proof that Linkgurus has delivered the new flagship; it becomes permission to use the founder’s history accurately.

Proof point B — one full-price authority path, end to end#

Sell خارطة الصلاحيات, capture the baseline, convert only if the diagnostic reveals a real authority problem, then deliver إرساء النموذج المؤسسي through enactment. The proof artifact must show at least two authority thresholds used in real decisions and a hold check. This is the indispensable pre-launch case because it proves the launch flagship itself.

Proof point C — one full-price Value on Hold engagement#

Sell القيمة المعلّقة to an organization where a significant investment went live without the intended operating change. Record the workflow/role collision, the organizational change accepted, and the value proxy the client agrees is legitimate. This tests the strongest white-paper-supported market need and the founder’s ability to convert enterprise-transformation credibility into a Linkgurus transaction.

Parallel experiment — Challenge Framing Workshop, classified honestly#

Run مجلس القرار when the content audience produces a genuine stuck decision. Its job is to test whether decision work is bought standalone, whether Arabic changes the room, and whether the decision holds. It is category and channel evidence, not proof of the Install. It should not be counted as one of two flagship proof points.

2.3 Minimum public-launch evidence gate#

Do not advance because a planned number of weeks has passed. Advance when all four are true:

  1. one إرساء النموذج المؤسسي engagement has reached an operating state;
  2. one second full-price paid engagement has been delivered—preferably القيمة المعلّقة, otherwise a second authority case;
  3. two clients permit referenceable proof, with at least one named publicly;
  4. the proof templates contain before, change, observed use, and hold—not only testimonials.

If founder bandwidth or the pilot result makes this take longer, the public launch moves. That is the purpose of the gate.

The key difference from Phase 12#

Phase 12 makes the Stage 1 website carry all three entry/flagship/scale ladders before a flagship is delivered. This alternative keeps the architecture visible but does not flatten availability or proof status. The website becomes a record of what is true at each stage, not a catalogue of everything designed in Phase 8.2.

4. Existing-client and network transition plan#

4.1 Transition principle#

Do not communicate a pivot. The name remains Linkgurus and the legacy site is treated as void, so the stable frame is: the firm has named and packaged the work it is equipped to do. The founder remains the credibility source; the corporate entity becomes clearer around her.

4.2 Language guidance#

4.3 Sequence of communication#

  1. Past clients first, privately. Ask permission to verify historical outcomes before asking for an introduction or a new sale.
  2. Five warm GCC contacts second. Use one diagnostic question and record whether the gap is knowledge, alignment, authority, or implementation.
  3. Alumni/referral network third. Ask for a specific introduction to a buyer facing one named trigger—not “anyone who might need consulting.”
  4. Owned list fourth. Promote one entry problem, not the entire portfolio. Track job title, geography, and problem type separately.
  5. Public audience last. Publish proof and a point of view. Do not announce internal strategy work as news.

D15 keeps Linkgurus, while Phase 9 finds the name carries little useful Arabic meaning and the existing equity sits on the founder. Therefore:

  • Kholoud remains the author and visible expert through the pilot and soft-transition stages.
  • Linkgurus is named as the firm she leads and the contracting entity—not as a separate content persona.
  • In Arabic, place «مَن يقرر… وماذا يبقى» next to the name wherever the name first appears. This follows Phase 9/10; it does not rely on the missing visual-identity files.
  • Do not create a parallel corporate publishing cadence until there is a second credible delivery voice or a firm-level audience of its own.

Risk interdependencies#

  • Breadth × bandwidth: a broad public menu produces enquiries the founder cannot qualify or deliver, making the brand appear inactive when content slows.
  • Price × proof: the less proof exists, the more buyers will force comparison to time or a familiar vendor. One discounted flagship then becomes the only market price in the firm’s history.
  • Arabic claim × personal brand: if Arabic is oversold as the differentiation, the market may attribute it to Kholoud’s personal platform rather than to a firm-level method.
  • AI demand × capability: strong research support for T3 increases temptation to sell early; that is precisely where Linkgurus’s approach proof and assurance boundary are thinnest.

6. Early success signals — first 90 days#

These are pre-registered decision rules, not forecasts. The founder should set final numeric thresholds before outreach using current pipeline, audience composition, and capacity. The working thresholds below are deliberately modest because the sample is small.

Interest and buying point to the same practice. The hooks that generate engagement also generate qualified conversations and paid work. 6.2 What would justify revisiting the Phase 8 territory choice#

Do not reopen the architecture because one post performs poorly or one buyer rejects the price. Revisit the lead territory if, after the agreed test volume:

  1. authority-language conversations do not convert to a paid diagnostic;
  2. the first authority diagnostics reveal no material enactment opportunity; and
  3. another entry offer—most plausibly القيمة المعلّقة—repeatedly produces stronger budget-holder pull.

That combination says the sequencing is wrong. It does not automatically say an offer should be killed or merged. Any portfolio change remains a founder decision.

6.3 The 90-day evidence ledger#

Track each interaction in one row: source, buyer title, geography, trigger, knowledge/alignment/authority/implementation gap, offer discussed, price comparator, next step, result, and what the observation validates. Without this ledger, the launch will generate anecdotes rather than learning.

Founder decisions and open inputs#

The roadmap can begin without answers to every item, but it cannot move through all stages safely without them.

  1. D7 visibility rule: may all nine offers remain in the architecture while only three are marked “available now”?
  2. D11/D13 pricing rule: full GCC price in Egypt, or a precisely defined pilot policy that does not become an improvised discount?
  3. Current pipeline, commitments, and weekly founder capacity: determines stage timing and the one-flagship cap.
  4. Cash runway: determines whether the price range can be held without pressure.
  5. KSA contracting route: direct, partner, subcontract, or another legally advised route.
  6. Reference permissions and outcomes: GISCON, the enterprise-transformation story, and any past-client result that can be verified.
  7. Delivery support: whether any associate can support research, documentation, change tracking, or quality assurance without representing the firm as a nonexistent bench.
  8. Missing Phase 11 visual files: restore them if they exist elsewhere, or remove every D24/logo dependency from Phase 12.

Final recommendation#

Approve Phase 12’s proof-before-publicity logic and first full flagship, but revise the operating roadmap before execution:

  1. correct the Phase 11 source references;
  2. bring القيمة المعلّقة into the first quiet-pilot wave;
  3. keep مجلس القرار as a paid falsification experiment, not flagship proof;
  4. require an enacted إرساء النموذج المؤسسي case before public launch;
  5. show portfolio availability honestly rather than publishing nine equal claims;
  6. retain prices as testable anchors, not research-validated rates;
  7. make T7 the evidence rail across every engagement and case study.

This is a sequencing correction, not a repositioning of the repositioning.
