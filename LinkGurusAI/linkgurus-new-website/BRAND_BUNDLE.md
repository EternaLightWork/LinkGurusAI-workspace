## 1. Chosen territory

### Final strategic choice

Linkgurus did **not** choose one territory and discard the rest. The final choice is a portfolio system:

- **One competence:** roles, job architecture, decision rights, authority and accountability.
- **Three practices:** The Lab; Restructuring & Org. Design; The Operating Model.
- **Seven territories:** all retained, but assigned different roles and proof gates.
- **One implementation promise:** T7 runs beneath every practice as a single descriptor line; it is not a standalone service or competing framework.
- **First full flagship path:** **خارطة الصلاحيات → إرساء النموذج المؤسسي → فحص الصلاحيات الدوري**.
- **First paid wave:** the three bounded entry offers — **خارطة الصلاحيات**, **القيمة المعلّقة** and, where a live decision exists, **مجلس القرار**.

The launch logic is therefore: **sell what is closest to current evidence, use entry offers to create measured baselines, complete one enacted flagship, then expand visibility and scale.**

### Binding territory rules

1. All seven territories remain represented; none is silently discarded.
2. Announced together does **not** mean presented as equally proven or equally available.
3. The authority ladder is the first full flagship path; the other two practices remain active through entry offers and content.
4. Practice 3 is **claimed in thought leadership before it is sold at flagship level**.
5. Every scale offer requires a baseline installed by its preceding flagship.
6. T7 is the evidence rail across every practice, not a fourth practice page.

### T7 — one promise beneath the portfolio

> **Organization design is not complete until it is applied and change is managed.**  
> **لا يكتمل التصميم المؤسسي إلا بالتطبيق وإدارة التغيير.**

T7 is not a tenth offer, a second delivery philosophy or a visible five-field framework. Within an engagement, each business result is assigned to a **specific named person from the client organization**.

### 6A — enterprise delivery path

6A is the delivery path for large enterprise projects. It is primarily housed under **Restructuring & Org. Design** and commercially led by **The Install — إرساء النموذج المؤسسي**. It is not a fourth practice, a tenth offer or a replacement for any offer.

| Stage | Final Arabic wording |
|---|---|
| Awaken | نكشف موضع التعطل |
| Assess | نثبت خط الأساس |
| AI Fit | هل يدخل الذكاء الآن؟ |
| Anchor | نثبت من يقرر وما الذي يتغير |
| Align | نُدخل التصميم في العمل |
| Ascend | نثبت ما تم تصميمه |

Large projects begin as restructuring and organization-design engagements. AI Fit is the third stage of the final sequence: **Awaken · Assess · AI Fit · Anchor · Align · Ascend**. It produces **Now** (embed AI in the same target operating model), **Later** (prepare interfaces, dependencies and a dated backlog) or **No** (continue restructuring without artificial AI content). AI does not become a parallel technology plan.

### Portfolio-wide commercial rules

- **Price the product, not founder-days.** A day rate returns the brand to the independent-consultant tier.
- **Entry offers are real paid products**, not free scoping calls and not an obligation to continue.
- **Facilitation is a delivery mechanism**, never the market category.
- **No total-rewards or salary-scale design** inside the organization-design scope.
- **No technical implementation, security assurance, legal opinion or regulatory assurance** inside the operating-model scope.
- **Capability transfer does not end the relationship:** the client's people run the work; Linkgurus retains a separately chosen review cycle. This recurring model is designed, not yet evidenced.

## 3. Brand name & verbal identity

### Name decision

**Keep `Linkgurus` as the corporate name.** The corporate brand is built on a promise, values and a documented way of working—not on one person. Linkgurus is the positioned firm and contracting entity.

The name itself signals connectivity and carries no useful Arabic meaning. The response is not to explain the name repeatedly; it is to make the verbal lockup and category architecture carry the positioning.

**Binding Arabic lockup:**

> **لينك جوروز**  
> **«مَن يقرر… وماذا يبقى»**  
> **المختبر · البنية والتصميم المؤسسي · نموذج التشغيل الذكي**

The wordmark never appears alone above the fold on an Arabic page. In first-contact Arabic correspondence, the corporate line follows the name immediately.

### Brand architecture and voice ownership

- Linkgurus speaks in the institutional voice of its promise, values and 6A way of working.
- Kholoud may be a visible expert and author, but the corporate descriptor, promise and credibility logic do not depend on naming her.
- Individual authorship can be credited on content without turning the corporate brand into a personal brand.
- Do not describe the launch as a rebrand, relaunch, pivot, new direction or new chapter. The frame is: **the underlying competence has been named, scoped and packaged.**

### Final bilingual thesis system

**01, commercial master promise**

> **Frame the AI challenge worth solving. Redesign the organization around it. Put the new way of working into operation.**
>
> **نُحدّد تحدّي الذكاء الاصطناعي الذي يستحق الحل، ونعيد تصميم المنظمة بما يلزم لتحقيقه، ثم نُدخل طريقة العمل الجديدة إلى الواقع.**

**02, provocative point of view**

> **The AI tool is not the transformation. The organization that can use it, govern it, and turn it into value is.**
>
> **أداة الذكاء الاصطناعي ليست هي التحوّل. التحوّل هو منظمة تعرف كيف تستخدمها، وتحكمها، وتحولها إلى قيمة.**

**03, short campaign and section line**

> **From the AI challenge worth funding to the operating model that makes it work.**
>
> **من تحدّي الذكاء الاصطناعي الذي يستحق الاستثمار إلى نموذج التشغيل الذي يجعله يعمل.**

Alternatives 04 and 05 are removed from the final system.

**Mission, الرسالة**

> **Make the hidden organization visible, and redesign it around value.** Linkgurus brings hidden organizational conditions into view, frames the challenges worth solving, and redesigns roles, authority, workflows and human-AI operating models so organizations can protect their real wealth and turn change into shared value.

**Vision, الرؤية**

> **Organizations where what matters is visible, and progress strengthens the whole system.** We envision organizations where what matters is visible, decisions have clear ownership, people and AI work within accountable systems, and growth strengthens business performance, people and community together.

### Tone of voice

| Quality | We sound like | We do not sound like |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise — دقيق** | A named owner, threshold, date and observable result | “Measurable outcomes,” “tangible impact” or unspecific transformation claims |

**Standing prohibitions:** no flattery, no superlatives about the firm, no implied third-party validation, no invented proof, no fee/guarantee/contingency language, and no generic consulting slogans.

## 4. Arabic messaging architecture

### Localized pillars

Arabic is not a translation layer. It is the source language, and several of the strongest distinctions exist only in Arabic.

| Pillar | Localized Arabic to ship | Register | Do not ship / localization rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| One competence, three situations | **«مَن يقرر… وماذا يبقى»**; descriptor: **«اختصاصٌ واحد… في ثلاث حالات»** | R3, spoken-adjacent MSA | Not «كفاءة واحدة، ثلاث غرف». “Rooms” does not transfer; كفاءة can mean efficiency or one qualified person. |
| A decision, not a report | **«قرارٌ… لا تقرير»** | R2, contemporary business MSA at its upper edge | Not «قرار، وليس تقريراً». Preserve the tanwīn on **قرارٌ** in display use; the balanced root construction is the point. |
| Work gets delegated; authority does not | **«تفويض العمل دون إرساء النموذج المؤسسي»** | R2, governance-inflected MSA | Not «يتم تفويض العمل. أما السلطة فلا». Avoid `يتم + verbal noun`; use الصلاحية, not political السلطة. |

### Logo concept direction — final

The final mark is an **open connector** with a defined opening and a small amber endpoint/dot. It is not the retired hexagonal network knot, and it is not an AI/neural symbol.

- **Latin:** mark sits on the left; its opening points inward toward the wordmark.
- **Arabic:** mark is mirrored to the right; its opening points inward toward the RTL wordmark.
- The Arabic wordmark is outlined paths, not live text, and is stacked `لينك` over `جوروز`.
- The blue form uses `#005CFF`; the final dot uses `#FBB400`.
- The mark may appear alone only where the name appears adjacent in text, such as an icon, footer or document header.

## 7. Flagship offer & proof points

### First full flagship — `إرساء النموذج المؤسسي / The Install`

**Practice:** البنية والتصميم المؤسسي / Restructuring & Org. Design  
**Entry:** خارطة الصلاحيات / Where Decisions Stop  
**Buyer:** CEO, owner or board sponsor with authority to change roles and decision rights  
**Trigger:** material decisions repeatedly return to one person; growth has made the authority system unusable; or an approved structure has not been enacted  
**Format:** fixed-price project with facilitated executive decisions inside it; neither a workshop product nor a retainer  
**Duration:** 10–14 weeks  
**Approved price anchor:** $55k–$90k / approximately SAR 205k–340k; market-unvalidated  
**Tail:** فحص الصلاحيات الدوري, offered only after the installed baseline and first hold check

### Core work and deliverables

1. **Authority baseline:** decision paths and latency for 2–3 material decisions.
2. **Role and decision architecture:** role charters, decision owners, approval thresholds, escalation and override rules.
3. **Job architecture:** roles, spans/layers and boundaries, excluding total rewards and salary-scale design.
4. **Enactment register:** what authority moved, from whom, to whom, when and on which live decision it was exercised.
5. **Operating evidence pack:** before/after measures, live decision examples, unresolved risks, observed use and next review date.
6. **Permissioned case-study record:** name, wording and outcome permission agreed separately in the SOW.

**Completion condition:** at least two material authority thresholds have moved, been used in live decisions and been observed against the baseline. “Board approved,” “org chart signed” or “report delivered” is not completion.

### Proof-before-scale plan

#### Proof point A — verify legacy credibility before public claims

- Quantify the **GISCON** outcome and obtain client confirmation if possible.
- Verify the founder's enterprise-transformation scope through a document or former colleague.
- Verify public wording, dates and scope for Geidea/SAP and ARASCO.

This permits accurate use of founder history. It does **not** become proof that Linkgurus delivered the new flagship.

#### Proof point B — one full-price authority path end to end

- Sell **خارطة الصلاحيات** at full price.
- Capture the decision-latency and authority baseline.
- Convert only if the map reveals a material authority problem.
- Deliver **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي** through enactment.
- Show at least two authority thresholds exercised in real decisions.
- Conduct and record a hold check.

This is the indispensable flagship case.

#### Proof point C — one full-price Value on Hold engagement

- Sell **القيمة المعلّقة** where a significant investment went live without the intended operating change.
- Record the workflow/role collision, agreed organizational change and a client-approved value proxy.

This tests the strongest externally supported entry need and creates the delivery bridge into the AI Operating Model.

#### Parallel paid experiment — Decision Council

- Run **مجلس القرار** only around a genuine stuck decision.
- Its job is to test whether decision work is purchased standalone, whether the alignment gap is real and whether the decision holds.
- It proves its own entry method; it does **not** prove the organization-design flagship or the AI Operating Model.

### Minimum public proof-launch gate

Before the full public proof launch:

1. One full flagship is completed to an operating state.
2. A second full-price paid entry engagement in another trigger is delivered.
3. At least two permissioned proof artifacts exist.
4. The founder credential story is verified or unsupported claims are removed.
5. The method/canvas provenance and the proof-measurement standard are documented.
