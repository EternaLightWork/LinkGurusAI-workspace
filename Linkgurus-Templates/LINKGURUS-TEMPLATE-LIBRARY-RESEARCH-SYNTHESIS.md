# Linkgurus Template Library — Research Synthesis, Source Map, and Bilingual Design Briefs

**Corpus freeze:** 17 August 2026  
**Miro export update:** 26 PDFs + 1 whole-board JPEG added, opened, rendered, and incorporated on 17 August 2026.  
**Evidence corpus:** 76 canonical sources. The former 26-URL MENA market / voice-of-customer register is excluded from inventory, categorization, adaptation evidence, and source counts; its only permissible use was early language calibration.  
**Portfolio held intact:** 3 practices, 9 offers, with T7 Implementation Accountability as the connective spine.  
**Purpose:** convert the collected corpus into traceable, original, designer-ready lead-magnet templates for Arabic-speaking MENA decision-makers.

## Reading protocol and assumptions

- **“Opened and read”** means the full local text was extracted/read, the local visual pages or workbook sheets were rendered and inspected, or the live webpage body was opened in the browser. A title or search snippet was never used as a substitute for an available source.
- **Unavailable means unavailable—but a later export supersedes an access failure.** The two live Miro URLs exposed only loading shells in the browser; the subsequently supplied raster exports were opened frame by frame and now supply primary visual evidence. The failed live captures remain in the register as access history.
- **Duplicates are not extra validation.** The five blog-export files contain the same 15 Design Sprint Academy articles in full, shortened, or differently formatted forms. The canonical reading set is `blogs_full.json`; duplicate exports are logged separately in the provenance audit but not counted as independent evidence.
- **The book file is a bibliographic pointer, not the book.** The official 24-page publisher sample and the authors’ official page were inspected; the complete book was not present, so only the public-sample mechanism is used.
- **Arabic is authored from the mechanism, not from the English copy.** Arabic uses contemporary business MSA, Gulf courtesy morphology, and the existing Linkgurus glossary. English is independently voiced for an executive reader.
- **Visual identity is governed by the current Linkgurus system.** The green/red/blue emojis identify the three practices architecturally; they are not approved brand colors. The templates use white, neutral ink, the Signal blue (`#005CFF` / deep `#0047C4`), and non-color encodings. Practice distinction comes from icon family, line pattern, and position—not traffic-light color.
- **No template is proof of client delivery.** The tools diagnose, structure, and qualify. They do not imply testimonials, client results, certifications, or proprietary ownership of third-party methods.

### Source-strength key

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Full primary mechanism source read directly: article, PDF, workbook, transcript, or official sample. |
| **B** | Complete local synthesis or Linkgurus governance source; useful but not independent market proof. |
| **X** | Inaccessible, empty, blocked, or only a pointer; no mechanism inferred. |

### Why this is an adaptation, not a collage

The board does **not** place nine borrowed canvases beside one another. ## 3A. 6A for very large restructuring-led projects

### The architectural decision

**6A should become the program-delivery path for complex enterprise engagements—not a fourth practice, a tenth offer, or a new item in the public offer grid.** Its primary home remains **البنية والتصميم المؤسسي**, with **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي / The Install** as the lead flagship. It can orchestrate modules from **نموذج التشغيل الذكي / The Operating Model** when an explicit mid-program AI Fit Gate is passed.

The relationship is:

| Layer | Role in a very large project |
|---|---|
| **The 3 practices / 9 offers** | Commercial modules the client can buy and understand. None is merged or removed. |
| **6A** | The engagement choreography across discovery, design, enactment, and learning. It tells the team **how the program moves**. |
| **T7** | The proof standard applied at every gate. It tells the client **what must be true before a stage is complete**. |

- **English:** **6A Enterprise Transformation Path — from structure to an operating state that holds**

### The AI Fit Gate

AI enters the project only when all seven questions have defensible answers. A “no” is a valid design decision, not a failed AI sale.

| Gate question | Pass evidence | If absent |
|---|---|---|
| **Which business result changes?** | One result and measure are named. | Continue restructuring; do not open an AI workstream. |
| **Is the workflow understood well enough?** | Current path, exceptions, handoffs, and decision points are visible. | Use Value on Hold or The Decision Lab to learn before designing the model. |
| **Is the value material?** | A credible value range or avoided loss exceeds the organizational change burden. | Place in Later/No; do not automate for theatre. |
| **Can required data and access be governed?** | Data source, permission, quality owner, and evidence retention are known. | Resolve the dependency or defer. |
| **Who owns every result?** | A named human accepts the result, exception, and escalation duty. | AI cannot enter the target state. |
| **Can the boundary be controlled?** | Recommend/decide/execute limit, prohibited actions, override, and audit evidence are explicit. | Restrict autonomy or defer. |
| **Can the organization absorb the change?** | Role capacity, transition sequence, capability, and sponsor decisions are funded and timed. | Continue structural work and revisit at a dated gate. |

| Program role | Existing offer used | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead commercial spine** | **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي / The Install** | The main contract and primary home for the complete 6A journey. |
| **Structural entry / evidence module** | **خارطة الصلاحيات / Where Decisions Stop** | Awaken–Assess when the baseline is not credible enough for direct flagship entry. |
| **AI value diagnostic module** | **Value on Hold — القيمة المعلّقة** | Assess, only where a live or proposed investment collides with roles/workflows. |
| **High-uncertainty decision module** | **مجلس القرار / Challenge Framing Workshop** or **مختبر القرار / The Decision Lab** | Before or at the AI Fit Gate when the decision or use case needs structured evidence. |
| **Conditional AI design workstream** | **نموذج تشغيل وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي / AI Operating Model** | Anchor–Align, only after a **Now** decision. |
| **Structural hold loop** | **فحص الصلاحيات الدوري / Standing Review** | Ascend after the new authority model is live. |
| **AI hold loop** | **سجل المسؤولية / Accountability Review** | Ascend only where an agent-enabled operating model was installed. |
| **Capability-transfer option** | **مختبركم / Your Lab** | Align–Ascend if the client must internalize decision-design facilitation. |

### Commercial and governance rule

The client may buy one **enterprise program**, but the proposal should show the existing offers as named modules with their own scope, evidence gate, owner, and commercial line. This preserves the nine-offer architecture and prevents three failures:

1. AI becoming a fashionable bolt-on to a nearly finished organization chart.
2. A traditional restructuring team designing roles that the later AI workstream immediately invalidates.
3. Linkgurus implying that every restructuring project must become an AI transformation.

The governing sentence is:

> **Start with the operating problem. Decide on AI after the baseline. If it enters, it enters the same target operating model—not a parallel technology plan.**

Arabic client-facing close:

> **نبدأ من موضع التعطل، لا من الأداة. وبعد تثبيت خط الأساس نقرر: هل يدخل الذكاء الآن، أم لاحقاً، أم لا يدخل؟ وإذا دخل، يدخل في التصميم نفسه لا في مسارٍ موازٍ.**

### Arabic naming decision for The Install

The proposed **التأسيس والتصميم** moves in the right direction because it signals a larger engagement than delegation. Its weakness is that it lists two broad activities, places establishment before design, and still does not promise launch, adoption, or change management. The recommended working name is **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي**: it names the target as a complete institutional operating model and uses **إرساء** to imply design made real and stable—not a document delivered.

| Arabic name | What it signals | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **إرساء النموذج المؤسسي — recommended** | A complete model is designed, installed, launched, and stabilized. Best semantic match for **The Install** and the full 6A journey. | Slightly formal; strongest for enterprise buyers. |
| **التصميم المؤسسي والتفعيل** | Explicitly combines design with activation. | Very clear, but more descriptive than ownable. |
| **بناء البنية وتشغيلها** | Direct promise: build the structure and make it work. | Strong and quotable, but less formal for government or board contexts. |
| **تأسيس البنية المؤسسية** | Closest refinement of **التأسيس والتصميم**; signals substantial change. | Can sound like greenfield company setup and understate launch. |
| **من التصميم إلى التشغيل** | Makes the journey and implementation gap unmistakable. | Strong campaign or program line; weaker as a standalone offer noun. |

The Arabic name should always be followed by the implementation line: **لا يكتمل التصميم المؤسسي إلا بالتطبيق وإدارة التغيير.**

### 4.9 Scale — سجل المسؤولية / Accountability Review

> **Source-strength flag:** B05 and B08 provide language and non-blaming ledger conventions, but the corpus contains no tested quarterly accountability-review mechanism. The two directions below are first-principles designs and must be validated in delivery before Linkgurus describes the artifact as proven.

**Two defensible directions**

1. **Recommended — Change-Led Agent Register:** start with what entered, changed, or retired since the last baseline; re-check owner, boundary, control evidence, and exceptions only where the estate changed.
2. **Alternative — Sampled-Result Audit:** sample actual agent results from the quarter and trace each to its human review, override, evidence, and escalation. Stronger assurance, but more operationally expensive and potentially outside Linkgurus’s non-technical boundary without specialist participation.

# 6. Designer Handoff Rules

1. **Build one component system, not nine art directions.** Every artifact uses the same header, metadata strip, evidence states, owner field, T7 rail, export pattern, and CTA module.
2. **Keep each main template recognizable in one glance.** No main artifact exceeds one landscape canvas. Supporting cards may be separate pages, maximum two per offer.
3. **Author Arabic first.** Layout is mirrored, not merely right-aligned. Arabic labels may occupy 15–20% more vertical space. No letterspacing, italics, faux bold, or truncated tashkīl.
4. **Use exact fonts and tokens.** Almarai 400/700/800; Instrument Sans 400/500/600/700; white, neutrals, `#005CFF`, and `#0047C4`; blue coverage around 10%.
5. **Encode diagnostic states without blame.** Held = solid; unassigned = empty; absent = hatch; in-question = blue inline rule. Never traffic-light colors.
6. **Preserve evidence type.** Every material claim can be marked Evidence / Inference / Assumption / Unknown. A numerical score never hides an unknown.
7. **Make the conversion contextual.** Export/request payload keeps language, trigger, template, offer, fields left blank, and user-selected next step. The visitor never retypes the diagnosis into a generic contact form.
8. **Do not claim validation where it is absent.** Where Decisions Stop, Standing Review, and Accountability Review launch as **Linkgurus beta instruments** until delivery produces referenceable evidence and version history.
9. **Protect provenance.** Metadata on every file: “Adapted by Linkgurus from the source mechanisms listed in the research register; wording, structure, decision rail, and MENA localization are original.” Do not imply affiliation with DSA, Strategyzer, Miro, Character Capital, or the authors of *Click*.
10. **Measure commercial performance by qualified movement.** Track completion, source/template, offer routing, qualified-call rate, Entry→Flagship conversion, and later Scale eligibility—not raw downloads alone.

## The library’s proprietary center
