# Linkgurus First-Month Buyer Outcomes

**Status:** final beta commercial plan  
**Internal commercial owner:** designated Linkgurus team member  
**Goal:** create the conditions for one full-price paid entry engagement in the first 30 days.  
**Integrity note:** [Unverified] No plan can guarantee one buyer in one month. The outcomes and actions below are commercial hypotheses to test, not forecasts.

---

## 1. The first-month commercial decision

Do not launch all nine offers as nine equal invitations.

Lead with one commercially sharp outcome:

> **Frame the AI challenge worth solving, make a defensible Now / Later / No decision, and name the organizational conditions required for it to create value.**

Arabic:

> **حدّد تحدّي الذكاء الاصطناعي الذي يستحق الحل، واتخذ قراراً واضحاً: الآن أو لاحقاً أو لا، وثبّت ما يجب أن يتغير في المنظمة حتى تتحقق القيمة.**

### Primary beta offer

**مجلس القرار / AI Challenge Framing Council**

- Buyer: CEO, Chief Strategy Officer, Chief Transformation Officer, or business-unit leader with an active AI investment decision.
- Trigger: several AI ideas/vendors are competing; leadership cannot defend which challenge should receive money now.
- Format: one working day plus pre-work and a signed decision/hold record.
- Business outcome: one challenge selected or rejected; investment logic made explicit; decision owner named; preliminary AI Fit decision recorded; organizational conditions and next route defined.
- Technology boundary: no technology selection, build, architecture, or technical assurance.
- Commercial status: paid beta; bounded; no free diagnostic.

### Why this is the first door

1. It makes AI explicit without turning Linkgurus into an AI-tool consultancy.
2. It uses Kholoud’s desired commercial wedge: challenge framing.
3. It can route naturally into any of the three practices.
4. It is smaller and faster to purchase than a flagship.
5. It demonstrates the Linkgurus distinction: AI is admitted only when the business challenge, value, owner, workflow, and change conditions are credible.

## 2. Three buyer outcomes to sell

### Outcome A — Decide the AI challenge worth funding

**Buyer language**

> “We have AI ideas, vendor proposals, and executive pressure—but no defensible decision about which business challenge should move first.”

**Promise**

> In one bounded Council, turn competing AI ideas into one defensible Now / Later / No decision, with a named owner, value hypothesis, evidence gaps, and the organizational conditions required before investment proceeds.

Arabic:

> في مجلس واحد محدد النطاق، تتحول أفكار الذكاء الاصطناعي المتنافسة إلى قرار يمكن الدفاع عنه: الآن أو لاحقاً أو لا، مع مسؤول بالاسم، وفرضية قيمة، وفجوات الدليل، والشروط المؤسسية اللازمة قبل الاستثمار.

**Required outputs**

- Challenge statement tied to a business/customer result.
- Options, including “do not proceed.”
- Decision criteria and evidence state.
- Preliminary AI Fit Gate: Now / Later / No.
- A specific named decision/result owner from the client organization.
- Routing decision:
  - decision remains the central problem → **مختبر القرار**;
  - structure/authority blocks movement → **خارطة الصلاحيات**;
  - a live investment/workflow collision exists → **القيمة المعلّقة**.

**Best first-month prospect**

- A GCC company with a 2026 AI mandate, three or more proposed use cases/vendors, and an executive meeting or budget deadline inside 30–60 days.

---

### Outcome B — Find why a live AI/digital investment has not released value

**Buyer language**

> “The system is live, usage is reported, but work, cycle time, customer value, or decision speed did not change.”

**Promise**

> In two weeks, identify where the investment collides with roles, handoffs, authority, information, or measures—and define the smallest organizational change that can begin releasing value.

Arabic:

> خلال أسبوعين، نحدد أين اصطدم الاستثمار بالأدوار أو التسليمات أو الصلاحيات أو المعلومات أو المقاييس، ونثبت أصغر تغيير مؤسسي يمكنه البدء في تحرير القيمة.

**Required outputs**

- One live workflow and its intended customer/business result.
- Collision map.
- Agreed value proxy and current baseline.
- Smallest organizational change.
- A specific named result owner from the client organization.
- Route to **نموذج تشغيل وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي** only when agents and genuine ownership/control questions exist.

**Best first-month prospect**

- COO or Transformation Director with a recent platform, automation, AI pilot, or operating change that went live without the expected operating result.

---

### Outcome C — Expose the authority condition blocking an AI or growth decision

**Buyer language**

> “The decision keeps returning upward, waits for one person, or crosses functions without a holder.”

**Promise**

> In one week, trace 2–3 material decisions, measure where they wait or return, and identify the authority change required before the organization can move.

Arabic:

> خلال أسبوع، نتتبع قرارين أو ثلاثة قرارات مؤثرة، ونقيس أين تنتظر أو تعود إلى الأعلى، ونحدد تغيير الصلاحية المطلوب حتى تتحرك المنظمة.

**Required outputs**

- Decision-latency baseline.
- Formal versus actual authority holder.
- Missing information/threshold/escalation rule.
- Priority authority change.
- A specific named result owner from the client organization.

**Best first-month prospect**

- Founder/CEO of a scaling company, or a business leader whose AI/transformation decision cannot pass through the current authority system.

## 3. First-month priority

| Priority | Buyer outcome | Offer | Commercial job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide the AI challenge worth funding | مجلس القرار | Primary AI + challenge-framing wedge |
| 2 | Release value from a live investment | القيمة المعلّقة | Strongest route when the buyer already has an implementation |
| 3 | Remove an authority blockage | خارطة الصلاحيات | Route for founder/CEO and structural problems |

Do not pitch all three in the same first message. Diagnose the live trigger, then present one outcome and one entry offer.

## 4. Minimum qualification

A first-month prospect is qualified only if all of the following are true:

- A real business decision, investment, or workflow exists now.
- An executive owns or influences the budget.
- There is a date or event creating movement within 30–60 days.
- The buyer will give access to the relevant business owners.
- The result can be described without generic “AI transformation” language.
- The buyer understands that Linkgurus implements the business organization around AI, not the technology.

Disqualify:

- “We want an AI awareness workshop.”
- “Show us tools” without a live business challenge or organizational decision.
- “Build us an agent.”
- No executive decision or deadline.
- No access to the work or decision owners.
- A request for a free diagnostic disguised as a meeting.

## 5. Beta acquisition path — 30 days

### Days 1–3 — prepare one sale

Create only these commercial materials:

1. One-page bilingual Council brief.
2. One-page “business implementation / technology implementation” boundary.
3. Decision in View / ورقة الحسم pre-read.
4. A two-page proposal template.
5. A short beta participation and evidence-permission clause.

Do not wait for the website.

### Days 4–10 — founder-led direct outreach

Build a list of 20 named prospects:

- 5 warm GCC executive contacts;
- 5 transformation/strategy leaders with active AI portfolios;
- 5 CEOs/founders experiencing authority bottlenecks;
- 5 former colleagues/clients who can introduce a specific active buyer.

Primary message:

> I am testing a bounded Linkgurus Council for leadership teams facing several AI bets but no defensible decision about which business challenge should move first. It does not build technology. It frames the challenge, runs a Now / Later / No AI Fit decision, recommends the relevant AI-tool category where useful, names the organizational conditions required, and assigns the business result to a specific person from the client. Is there one live decision in your organization—or someone you know—that must be resolved in the next 30–60 days?

Arabic:

> أختبر حالياً مجلساً محدد النطاق من لينك جوروز للفرق القيادية التي أمامها عدة رهانات في الذكاء الاصطناعي من دون قرار يمكن الدفاع عنه حول التحدّي الذي يجب أن يبدأ أولاً. لا نبني التقنية؛ نُحدّد التحدّي، ونحسم ملاءمة دخول الذكاء: الآن أو لاحقاً أو لا، ونوصي بفئة الأدوات المناسبة عند الحاجة، ونثبت الشروط المؤسسية المطلوبة، ثم نُسند النتيجة إلى شخص محدد بالاسم من جهة العميل. هل لديكم قرار فعلي يجب حسمه خلال الثلاثين إلى الستين يوماً القادمة، أو تعرفون قائداً يواجه هذا الموقف؟

### Days 11–17 — qualification conversations

Use five questions:

1. What business/customer result is under pressure?
2. What AI or transformation decision must be made?
3. What happens if no decision is made in the next 30–60 days?
4. Who owns the decision and budget?
5. Is the gap knowledge, alignment, authority, workflow, or implementation?

Route:

- knowledge/tool-selection gap → not Linkgurus;
- alignment/defensibility gap → Council;
- authority gap → Authority Map;
- live value/workflow collision → Value on Hold.

### Days 18–24 — propose one bounded paid engagement

Proposal structure:

1. Live decision/trigger in the buyer’s words.
2. Result of the engagement.
3. Scope and exclusions.
4. Inputs and executive access required.
5. Working session and pre/post work.
6. Deliverables.
7. Application/change promise and a specific named result owner from the client.
8. Fixed beta fee and payment terms.
9. Expiry date linked to the buyer’s real decision date.

Do not add a free discovery phase.

### Days 25–30 — close or learn

Success signal:

- one signed, full-price paid entry engagement;
- or a documented reason the purchase did not happen that changes the offer, buyer, trigger, channel, or price hypothesis.

Do not count:

- compliments;
- LinkedIn engagement;
- “send me a deck”;
- a free workshop;
- an introduction with no live trigger;
- interest from practitioners without budget or executive access.

## 6. First-month content outcomes

Publish no general “future of AI” content.

Use three founder-led pieces:

1. **The wrong first question:** “Which AI tool should we buy?”  
   Better question: “Which business challenge deserves AI—and what must change in the organization if the answer is Now?”

2. **A pilot can work while the organization cannot use it.**  
   Show the gap between technical success and roles, authority, workflow, and value.

3. **Every agent result needs a human owner with time and authority.**  
   Naming a sponsor is insufficient if the person cannot intervene.

Every piece routes to one of the three buyer outcomes—not to a generic services page.

## 8. Non-negotiable boundary language

**English**

> Linkgurus implements the organizational changes required for AI to work in the business: challenge and value framing, roles, authority, workflows, human ownership, controls, change, adoption, and business accountability. The client’s IT function or technology partner owns technology architecture, build, integration, security, deployment, and technical operations.

**Arabic**

> تتولى لينك جوروز تطبيق التغييرات المؤسسية اللازمة لعمل الذكاء الاصطناعي داخل المؤسسة: تحديد التحدّي والقيمة، وتصميم الأدوار والصلاحيات ومسارات العمل والمسؤولية البشرية والضوابط والتغيير والتبنّي والمساءلة عن النتائج. ويتولى فريق تقنية المعلومات لدى العميل أو شريكه التقني معمارية التقنية وبناءها وربطها وتأمينها وإطلاقها وتشغيلها التقني.
